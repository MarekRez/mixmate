# Data sources

This file records where the data of MixMate comes from.

## Recipes and ingredients

`src/assets/data/cocktails.json` and `ingredients.json` were written for this project. The cocktails come mainly from the official cocktail list of the IBA (International Bartenders Association). The recipes were written from the AI's knowledge of the IBA recipes and were **not** compared with the IBA website, so spot-check them before relying on them. Gin and Tonic is a classic that was added for beginners and is not on the IBA list, to the best of our knowledge.

## Cocktail images

The images in `src/assets/images/cocktails/` were downloaded from Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons: the lead image of the English Wikipedia article of each cocktail, in a 600 px wide version (or another Commons photo where that image was unsuitable). The table lists the Commons file page of every image, its author and its licence.

The project is a school project and the app is not published, so the licences were not checked beyond recording them (see SPEC.md, "Out of scope"). Before publishing the app, the attribution that CC BY and CC BY-SA require must be shown in the app, and the images must be re-checked.
